About Me

I developed an interest in coding about two years ago after watching a TikTok video about a girl who transitioned from Human Sciences to Tech after a period of unemployment. That one TikTok video sparked my curiosity, and I decided to explore the world of coding. Since then, I have immesred myself in the world of web development by attending tech summits, joining tech communities and short courses. I currently have a solid foundation in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I am eager to continue learning and growing as a developer. My long term interests include expanding into software testing and data analytics. I enjoy the fact that the tech industry is constantly evolving, as there is always something new to learn and improve on. Beyond the code, I bring strong accountability and discipline to development teams. I treat project management as a core developer skill, consistently using structured frameworks like the Eisenhower Matrix,time batching, and the PARA method to organize sprints and meet deadlines efficiently. As a collaborator, I thrive on open communication, active problem solving, and keeping team workflows running smoothly.
